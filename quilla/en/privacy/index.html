Quilla Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026

Quilla ("the App") is an app for Mac, iPhone and iPad provided by Taizo Yamaoka ("the Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. The Developer does not collect your data

The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your manuscripts, settings and usage are never sent to the Developer. The Developer does not operate any server for the App.

2. Where your manuscripts are stored

Manuscripts, images and other files are stored on your device and in your own iCloud Drive. iCloud storage and sync are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you add a folder of your own choosing (an external folder), the App reads and writes files in that folder.

3. Subscriptions (in-app purchases)

Purchases, renewals and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store. Neither the App nor the Developer receives your payment information.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The App asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only when you use dictation. Speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device when on-device recognition is available. Otherwise, audio may be sent to Apple's servers for recognition.

5. Preview features (third-party AI services)

Only if you turn on "Try Preview Features" in Settings and enter your own API key, the App sends data directly from your device to the service you chose. With the setting off (the default), nothing is sent.

FeatureWhat is sentWhere (the service you chose)
AI voice narrationThe text being read aloudElevenLabs / OpenAI / Microsoft Azure / Google Cloud / Fish Audio
Reading AIThe text being read aloudOpenAI
Illustration generationThe selected textOpenAI / Google (Gemini)

Data you send is handled under your agreement with that service and its privacy policy. Depending on the service and your plan, what you send may be used to train AI models. Please check each service's terms. The data does not pass through, and is never received by, the Developer.

API keys are stored only in your device's Keychain and are never sent to the Developer.

6. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with its effective date.

8. Contact

Please contact us via the support page.
